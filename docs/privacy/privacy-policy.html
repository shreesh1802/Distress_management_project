Privacy Policy

Road Inspection App · Last updated: 23 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Road Inspection app and its server collect, why, who can see it, and how you can ask us to delete it. The app is used by authorised personnel to inspect roads, record road-safety audits and manage project documents.

The Road Inspection App is published by Dr. Rohit Ahuja.

2. Device permissions

  • Camera: used only for Live Detection. You can refuse it; the rest of the app still works.
  • Microphone: used only to record audit commentary. You can refuse it and upload an audio file instead.
  • Files / storage: used only when you choose a file to upload.
  • Internet: required to talk to our server.

3. Who can see your information

  • Project members and administrators. Data you add to a project (surveys, audits, audio, documents) can be seen by the administrators of the system and by the other members assigned to that project. Some older inspection data (videos, detections, reports) is shared between all signed-in users of the same deployment.
  • Service providers that help us run the app (see section 4).
  • Legal or safety requests. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

4. Third parties that handle data

The app contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs and no crash-reporting services from third parties.

5. How long we keep your information

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. If your account is closed, or you ask us to delete your data, we delete or anonymise it within 30 days, except where we must keep something to meet a legal obligation or to protect the security of the service. Backups are overwritten on their normal cycle.

6. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, or delete your account and associated personal data. To make a request, email us at the address below from the email address registered to your account. We may need to verify your identity first.

Delete your account in the app: open the project list, tap the “Delete account” icon next to Sign out, and type your email address to confirm. Your account, sign-in sessions and pending login codes are deleted immediately. You can also email road.project@thapar.edu with the subject “Delete my account” from your registered address; we will complete the deletion within 30 days. More detail is on the account deletion page.

Data you added to a shared project (for example survey reports) may be retained in anonymised form so that other members’ records stay complete.

7. Security

  • The app talks to our server over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Sign-in uses one-time codes sent to your email, with limits on repeated attempts. Session tokens are stored hashed on our server and can be revoked by signing out.
  • Access to project data is restricted to the people who have been given access.

No system is completely secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us right away.

8. Children

The app is intended for professional use by adults. It is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. If a change is significant we will tell you in the app or by email.
