Delete your account

Road Inspection App · Published by Dr. Rohit Ahuja

Option 1: delete it in the app (immediate)

  1. Sign in to the Road Inspection app.
  2. On the project list screen, tap the Delete account icon next to Sign out (on larger screens: profile menu → Delete Account).
  3. Type your email address to confirm and tap Delete account.

Deletion takes effect straight away and cannot be undone.

Option 2: ask us to delete it

If you can no longer use the app, email road.project@thapar.edu with the subject “Delete my account”, from the email address registered to your account. We will verify the request and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Your account (email address, name)Deleted
Sign-in sessions and pending login codesDeleted
Your membership of projectsRemoved
Surveys, audits, audio commentary, documents and reports you added to a shared projectKept so that other project members’ records stay complete, but no longer linked to you (shown as “Deleted user”). To have specific items removed as well, email us and say which.
Security logs (IP address, request time)Rotate out on the hosting provider’s normal log cycle
BackupsOverwritten on their normal cycle
